# Design and architecture

Crescent Lake is an illustrated landscape, not a simulation of a surveyed place. A fixed, elevated orthographic camera frames a curved grassy bank, open water and one white swan. Palette, shape and restrained motion establish the visual style.

## Modules

| Module | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| `world.ts` | Camera, lighting, route, shared clock and scene lifecycle |
| `landscape.ts` | Procedural bank, tiled instanced grass and articulated trees |
| `water.ts` / `motion.ts` | Planar reflection, wind waves and world-space wake history |
| `swan.ts` / `atmosphere.ts` | Attached wing gestures, clouds and flying birds |
| `materials.ts` / `painterly.ts` | Palette lighting and selectable shader treatments |
| `music.ts` | Original score, synthesis, stereo space and prepared loop audio |
| `presentation.ts` | Shared typography and paper framing for screen and export |
| `master-export.ts` / `scripts/lossless-export.ts` | Deterministic frame uploads and local lossless encoding |

All scene movement uses the same clock. A circuit lasts 24 seconds; a cosine light curve reaches Golden Hour at 12 seconds and returns to Morning at 24. Integer-cycle motion and circular noise sampling close the animated details. Wake points retain their original world positions and headings.

The swan asset is generated by `scripts/create_bird.py` and distributed as a self-contained GLB. Running the app does not require Blender. Grass, trees, clouds, birds and music are created in code; fonts and the model are local assets.

## References

- [craftzdog / ghibli-style-shader](https://github.com/craftzdog/ghibli-style-shader): palette-based stylized lighting.
- [Stylized Components](https://github.com/cortiz2894/stylized-components): grass, water and rendering-system studies.
- [Three.js](https://threejs.org/): rendering, curves, Reflector and post-processing infrastructure.

The project does not include imagery, video, models or unpublished source from @notjignes. Physical fluid simulation, surveyed shorelines and a skeletal neck rig are outside its scope.
